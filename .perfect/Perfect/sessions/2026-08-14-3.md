## Phase P — the re-verification pass IS the proposal work

r23's hardest-won rule: **a bank note is a LEAD, never a finding.** Two Explore scouts were launched
— one per *intended lot*, so the scouting boundary and the build boundary are the same line — and
both were prompted to **REFUTE**, not to confirm.

**Result across the six banked claims: 4 CONFIRMED-and-SHARPER, 1 NARROWED with a sub-claim
REFUTED, 1 with its specified mechanism REFUTED as unbuildable.** Every one of the six changed in
some way. The three that changed *materially*:

1. **`hackernews-snapshot-floor`'s floor was unbuildable as written.** The note specified a floor
   against "what the page published"; HN publishes no total. The scout found the denominator the
   parser computes and throws away (`tr.athing` rows enumerated, then silently `filter_map`ped), so
   the direction survives with a different, buildable denominator. Had this not been caught, the
   builder would have discovered it mid-flight or, worse, shipped a floor against a fabricated total.
2. **`catalog-disarm-is-visible`'s premise was half wrong.** "There is no staleness marker" is
   false — `checked_at` and `job_id` already ride on every verdict row. The real defect is that no
   consumer derives anything from them, while the identical `age_secs`/`stale` idiom sits ~45 lines
   away *in the same function*. The direction was reframed onto `contracts_enforce` reporting
   configured **intent** rather than observed **enforcement**.
3. **`checkpoint-failures-metric` was rejected in r23 as observability-only, and that reasoning
   died.** The scout found the shutdown-suspend arm logs *"re-queued to resume from checkpoint"* —
   a durability assertion false exactly when `storage_error > 0`, with the sink in scope two lines
   above. It now closes a live defect rather than adding a view of one. Its sizing objection died
   too: `.announcing` is an existing `#[must_use]` builder that already hands an `AppState` `Arc`
   into the checkpointer.

**Director verification of the two severest claims, in source, before gating** — and one came out
sharper than its scout:

- **eu-sedia** (`lib.rs:185-240` read directly): with `totalResults` renamed, `total = 0`

- **the crawl pin** (`datasets.rs:1976-2002`, `crawl/src/lib.rs:575-587`/`:665-682`,
