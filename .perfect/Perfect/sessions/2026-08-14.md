## The trick that closed the sweep: two scouts for seven contexts

Repeating r21's family-scout lesson at maximum leverage. The seven never-proposed contexts were
covered by **two** scouts, not seven:

1. **A six-context "thin app + its catalog row" family brief.** `crates/core/src/catalog.rs` is the
   shared spine — the GitOps reconciler that all these thin apps declare into — so the interesting
   failures live at the *seam* between what an app emits and what its `[[source]]` row claims. Six
   per-context scouts would each have seen half of every defect. Covered `data-pipeline-catalog`,
   `connector-api-watch`, `czech-procurement`, `hackernews-example`, `page-monitor`,
   `us-state-grants`.
2. **A `wasm-plugin-examples` scout.**

**The family read produced a finding no per-context scout could have:** only 2 of the 6 apps have a
catalog row at all, and the other four are `CATALOG_EXEMPT` **by design and with documented
reasons** — so "missing catalog row" is not the drift story. The story is what the exemption
*costs*: no `[source.contract]` tripwire and no `/catalog/health` freshness row. And the one app in
the family that can tombstone at scale is on that exempt list.

## What the builders found that I did not brief

Both lots returned refinements, which is the signal the briefs were specs and not scripts:

1. **ca-grants had a SECOND lie in the same arm**, which neither I nor the scout named:
   `offset >= total` counts rows **requested**, so a page that asked for 1000 and delivered 100
   still advanced the offset by 1000 — on page 2 of a 1366-record corpus, `2000 >= 1366` called
   1100 records a complete sweep. Coverage is now counted on records **collected**.
2. **smlouvy's `year_from` interacts with the floor.** The builder moved the year filter to a
   borrowed `tracked` view so the floor is judged on the *parse*, never on the intentional filter —
   otherwise a deliberate `{"year_from": 2024}` would have looked like a partial parse and
   permanently suppressed removals. I did not specify this; it is the correct call.
3. **`let end;` declared uninitialised** in ca-grants so the compiler proves the reported sweep arm
   and the actual break cannot disagree. A default would have been "a second, silent opinion about
   how the walk ended".
4. Lot A chose **floor = 1.0** and argued it from asymmetry (a suppressed removal costs a stale
   pointer; a wrong tombstone costs a consumer its whole index) plus the source's nature
   (machine-generated, schema-stable since 2016), and kept the tombstone path reachable with a
   dedicated counter-test.
