what may be *named*, not whether it is *checked*, and adds the rule that makes it safe (empty cron,
asserted rather than trusted). Lot V likewise reported the `fetch_chokepoint.rs` scanner guard
rather than applying it, because that file was Class C.

the namespace message. A guard that has never been seen to fail is not yet a guard.
