   works" is wrong: `e2e/fetch_proxy.rs:277` binds a real listener, points a real `RemoteEngine`

   proven; the failure paths and the trust gap are what is missing. Slate reshaped accordingly —
   no direction was written to "prove the fabric works", because it already is proven.
